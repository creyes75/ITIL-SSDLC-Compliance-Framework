# IT Governance & SSDLC Framework (Terminal Terrestre Project)

This repository documents a strategic IT consultancy project delivered to the **Terminal Terrestre** (Regional Bus Transportation Regulatory Agency). The project was executed in partnership with **MIRFAK**, focusing on aligning IT operations with international standards for service management and security.

---

### 🎯 Project Objectives

The consultancy aimed to modernize the agency's IT governance by implementing two core pillars:
1.  **ITIL Integration:** Streamlining IT Service Management (ITSM) to improve uptime and service delivery for critical transportation infrastructure.
2.  **SSDLC Implementation:** Establishing a **Secure Software Development Life Cycle** to ensure all regulatory software tools meet high-security standards and data protection compliance.

### 🛠️ Key Deliverables & Contributions

*   **AS-IS Diagnosis:** Comprehensive assessment of existing IT processes and security gaps.
*   **Service Catalog Design:** Definition of ITIL-based services for internal and external stakeholders.
*   **Secure Development Standards:** Creation of a roadmap for secure coding, testing, and deployment (SSDLC).
*   **Policy Documentation:** Development of formal procedures for Change Management, Incident Management, and Risk Mitigation.
